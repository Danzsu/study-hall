DIFFICULTY MODIFIER: EASY (Tanulási mód)

Apply these rules on top of the base generator instructions:

- Focus on definitions, basic concepts, and memorization ("Mi az X?", "Mit jelent Y?")
- Prefer single correct-answer multi_choice questions
- Distractors should be clearly distinguishable from the correct answer (not tricky)
- Prefer true_false questions for straightforward factual claims
- Avoid multi-step reasoning or application questions
- Fill-in-the-blank answers should be single well-known terms (not numbers or formulas)
- If a concept has a commonly used Hungarian name, use that in the question
- Question length: keep titles short and direct
- Aim for roughly: 50% multi_choice, 30% true_false, 10% fill, 10% input
- Do not generate drag_n_drop questions in easy mode
